# Ca La Kimberly days: brief (from discussion, 11 Oct 2026, nothing built yet)

## Offer
- Rent the house and garden for the day (about 10:00 to 21:00) for a calçotada, a paella or a barbecue. No overnight stay (that stays on Airbnb).
- Included: barbecue and wood-fire area, covered patio, garden, pool (not heated, best May to October), living room with TV and board games for bad weather, kitchen and tableware. Bedrooms stay closed. Dogs welcome, kids can play in the garden.
- Possible extras: calçotada kit (calçots, romesco, vine shoots, newspaper, bibs), paella pan and burner, firewood, charcoal, towels, cleaning after.

## Size (decided by the house, set by the worst day: rain puts everyone in the living room)
- Comfortable for 6, works for 8. Paella / seated meal: 10 maximum. Calçotada standing: maybe 12 in good weather only.
- Cheap upgrades if needed: folding table and chairs for the lawn, patio heater or shade sail, a second burner or grill.

## Price (proposal, to test)
- Our cost per rental (estimate): cleaning 3 to 4 h or 60 to 80 EUR, two round trips from L'Hospitalet 20 to 30 EUR, utilities and wear 20 to 40 EUR. About 100 to 150 EUR, plus being displaced.
- Benchmark: restaurant calçotada menus in Valls about 43 to 46 EUR per person, food and wine included. Our guests also buy their food (about 10 to 15 EUR each).
- Proposal: off-peak weekdays 250 EUR; weekends and calçot season 350 EUR; summer weekends with pool 400 EUR. Up to 8 included, 25 EUR per extra person up to 10. Refundable deposit about 200 EUR. Extras priced separately with margin.
- Launch at 350 EUR on weekends; raise to 400 if it fills easily.
- Best days: when we would be away anyway or already driving there to clean between Airbnb stays.

## To check before the first booking
- Town hall of La Bisbal del Penedès: is a paid day of events allowed under our tourist licence (HUT, 7 people overnight)?
- e-fiscalidad: tax treatment (VAT, need to register as autónomo again?).
- Insurance: event liability, pool rules.
- Fire: lighting fires in or near forest land is banned in Catalonia 15 March to 15 October; check whether the fixed barbecue is allowed in summer.
- Neighbours: end time, music low after 20:00.
- Calendar: Airbnb calendar is the master; a day booking blocks Airbnb; keep cleaning time before check-ins.

## Low-risk first test
- Offer the extras (calçotada kit, paella kit) to our Airbnb guests through the guide first: no new legal or insurance questions, and it shows whether people pay for them.

## Marketing
- Audience: small groups from Barcelona and Tarragona (families, birthdays, friends' calçotadas).
- Channels: Instagram and TikTok (fire, pool), Google Ads ("masia calçotada lloguer dia"), Swimmy in summer.
- Track cost per request and per confirmed booking from day one.
