# Day rental: open legal and pricing questions (11 Oct 2026)

Status: first pass. Web research only. Nothing here is legal or tax advice, and the "to confirm" items need a call or email before the first paid booking.

## Pricing

### Decision needed: the mockup says 500, the brief says 250 / 350 / 400
The mockup hero shows "€500 per day, up to 10". The brief proposes 250 (off-peak weekday), 350 (weekend and calçot season), 400 (summer weekend with pool). The landing page I built follows the brief, launching at 350 on weekends.

### Why 500 is too high
Benchmark: a calçotada menu in Valls is about 43 to 46 EUR per person, food and wine included. Our guests also buy their own food (10 to 15 EUR each).

| Group | Price | House per head | Plus food | Total per head | vs restaurant 43 to 46 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 350 | 58 | 12 | 70 | +24 to 27 |
| 8 | 350 | 44 | 12 | 56 | +10 to 13 |
| 10 (350 + 2 x 25) | 400 | 40 | 12 | 52 | +6 to 9 |
| 10 | 500 | 50 | 12 | 62 | +16 to 19 |

At 350, a group of 8 to 10 pays about 10 EUR more than a restaurant and gets the pool, the fire, the dog, the privacy and no time limit. At 500 the gap is 16 to 19 EUR per head. Groups of 6 are the weak spot at any price.

### Margin (our cost estimate 100 to 150 EUR per rental)
| Price | Margin before tax | If 10% VAT is included | If 21% VAT is included |
|---|---|---|---|
| 250 | 100 to 150 | 227 net, margin 77 to 127 | 207 net, margin 57 to 107 |
| 350 | 200 to 250 | 318 net, margin 168 to 218 | 289 net, margin 139 to 189 |
| 400 | 250 to 300 | 364 net, margin 214 to 264 | 331 net, margin 181 to 231 |

The cost estimate does not price your time or the Airbnb night it may block.

### Recommendation
1. Launch at 350 on weekends and calçot season, 250 weekdays, 400 summer weekends, as in the brief.
2. Sell it to groups of 8 to 10. Say "up to 8 included, 25 EUR per extra person up to 10".
3. Decide whether the displayed price includes VAT before publishing (see Tax). The page does not mention VAT yet, on purpose, until that is settled.
4. Test 400 on the first two weekends only if requests fill within a week.
5. Deposit about 200 EUR, refundable.

## Legal and practical questions

### 1. Tourist licence (HUT) and a paid day of events
Status: not answered by what I found.
- Your licence is a HUT for up to 7 people overnight. I found no source that says whether it covers, forbids or ignores paid daytime events. The Catalan HUT rules are about overnight stays.
- A day rental for a party is a different activity from a stay. A town hall can treat it as an event, a venue hire or an unlicensed activity, and the answer can differ by municipality.
- To confirm: ask the Ajuntament de la Bisbal del Penedès, in writing, whether renting the house for a day (up to 10 people, 10:00 to 21:00, no overnight) needs a different licence or a communication (comunicació prèvia), and whether the HUT limit of 7 people applies to daytime guests.
- Until answered, I would not take payment. The page works as a request form, which fits the mockup ("you send a request, we confirm within 24 h").

### 2. Fire
Status: partly answered, from a 2011 article about Decret 64/1995, so confirm it is still current.
- From 15 March to 15 October no fire may be lit on forest land, or within 500 m of it, for any purpose.
- Fixed masonry barbecues with a spark arrester on urbanisation plots were allowed in that article. Whether yours counts is not something I can see from here.
- High-risk weather days can ban everything, including gas barbecues, and the town hall can add its own ban.
- Consequence: 15 October is in 4 days. Calçot season (November to April) is outside the ban, so the launch window is clean. Summer is the risk. Paella on a gas burner rather than a wood fire is the safer summer offer.
- To confirm: ask the Ajuntament and Agents Rurals whether the fixed barbecue can be used in summer, and what the rule is on very high risk days. Put a fire clause in the booking terms either way.

### 3. Tax (e-fiscalidad)
Status: my reading only, from a secondary source.
- Plain housing rental is exempt from VAT. Rental with hospitality-type services (cleaning, meals, welcome products) is taxed at 10%, according to a tax blog I fetched, not an official page.
- A day hire for a party is not housing use, so it is probably not the exempt case. I would assume VAT applies and ask which rate.
- You stopped being autónomo in April 2026. Whether regular day rentals need you to register again for IAE, VAT and social security, or whether it can run under another structure, is a question for e-fiscalidad. The extras (kits, cleaning) are probably the most clearly taxable part.
- To confirm: rate, registration, whether the displayed price must include VAT, and invoicing.

### 4. Insurance
Status: not researched.
- To confirm with your home insurer: event or third-party liability for paying guests, the pool (drowning, children), and fire from the barbecue. Ask whether the policy is void when the house is rented without an overnight stay.

### 5. Neighbours
Status: decision, not research.

### 6. Calendar
- Airbnb calendar stays the master. The day page should read it, or you block days by hand at first. A day booking blocks the Airbnb night. Keep cleaning time before check-ins.
